## Cross-Episode Synthesis

**Recurring good**
- Evidence hedging has improved noticeably (10/17, 10/15, 10/11). Hosts routinely say "our sources don't say" or "unverified," in line with the Community Content Compact.
- Casey's one-line aphorisms land every episode: "A binder doesn't get a driver," "A reference... a receipt," "The shepherd didn't get a church."
- The recurring anchor ("Who gets to leave, and who has to stay?") is a real editorial idea, and 10/16 and 10/17 execute it best.

**Recurring bad**
1. **Deep Dives are stitched, not anchored.** 10/13, 10/11, 10/12, and 10/17 each cover four to six unrelated items despite the prompt's explicit "ANCHOR ON ONE QUESTION" rule. The producer's "topics meandered" (10/17) and "meh" (10/14) match this.
2. **Local anchoring arrives late and often generic.** The Cariboo shows up in the last 15 to 20% of most Deep Dives, usually as "a town like Quesnel." Only 10/17 names real Cariboo entities consistently.
3. **Riley concedes too easily.** "Fine," "Right," and "Careful is right" appear in almost every episode, so the disagreement is shallower than the written setup promises.
4. **The "who maintains it in year five?" tic** appears in 10/12, 10/13, 10/14, and 10/15. It is a good question, but used mechanically it becomes a verbal habit.
5. **Land acknowledgements are template-locked.** "Reaching you from the heart of the Cariboo, the homeland of the Secwépemc, Tŝilhqot'in, and Dakelh nations" appears nearly verbatim in at least four episodes. Only 10/14 ("since time immemorial") varies it.
6. **Closing lines recycle.** "Have a great rest of your day" and "check the X before you trust it" repeat across episodes.
