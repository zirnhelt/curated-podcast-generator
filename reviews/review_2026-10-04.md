## Improvement Action Plan

1. **Gate unresolved allegations about named candidates before broadcast.**
 - **Problem:** 10/03 airs the Nelson civil petition ($15M, forged signatures) with status "not in today's sources," two weeks before a vote; 9/29 airs the 2021 Clare "guillotine" item, irrelevant to its theme.
 - **Lever:** `script_generation` and `script_generation_system` in `config/prompts.json`; plus a post-generation check in `podcast_generator.py` before the script is finalized.
 - **Change:** Add a rule: *"Do not report unresolved legal allegations, lawsuits, or old controversies about a named living person unless (a) the source article is dated within 14 days, (b) the current status is stated in the source, and (c) it is directly relevant to the day's theme. If status is unknown, omit."* Add a lightweight pass in `podcast_generator.py` that flags sentences containing named-person + terms like "alleges / petition / lawsuit / forged / misappropriated / guillotine" and routes them to a human-review log (or strips them) before audio render.
 - **Signal:** No named-person allegation appears without a stated current status and a within-14-day source; 10/03-style candidate coverage sticks to platform, record in office, and filed-candidate facts.
