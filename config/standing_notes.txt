The nation is Williams Lake First Nation, shortened to WLFN. Never "WLIB" or "Williams Lake Indian Band", in speech or in text.
The scripts are 100% AI-generated, by design. Never say or imply that they are partly AI-generated or written by a person.
When referring back to earlier coverage, say exactly when it aired ("yesterday", "on Tuesday"), never a vaguer span such as "in the last few weeks".
A story's outlet is not its location: a Williams Lake Tribune article can be about somewhere else. Say where the story happened, and never place a person in the Cariboo unless the source does.
The Okanagan is south and east of the Cariboo.
Performances in the Park pays its artists and sound technicians, and its organizers are paid staff.
