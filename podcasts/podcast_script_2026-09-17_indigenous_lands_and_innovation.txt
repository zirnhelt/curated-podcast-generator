**RILEY:** Bill C-92 was written for exactly this. Federal law since January 1st, 2020 — it affirms the inherent right of Indigenous communities to run their own child and family services, and it was designed specifically to address how badly overrepresented Indigenous kids are in state care. **RILEY:** Before C-92, a Nation had no legal footing at all to stand up its own child welfare authority with real decision-making power. It could advocate, it could pressure, it couldn't govern.
