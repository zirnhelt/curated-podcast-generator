Gemini multi-speaker timed out; the episode split voices

The text-to-speech render for the "news" section failed on Gemini's multi-speaker model after 4 attempts. The error was a read timeout from Google's API. The pipeline fell back to single-speaker OpenAI for the remaining sections and credits, leaving the already-rendered portions in their original voices.

This creates an audible seam. Casey and Riley's voice ratio across the episode is 0.98, but that balance holds only for the sections that made it through Gemini. The news segment and everything after it will sound different to the listener.

Script hit target after an expand pass

The first draft came in at 2,341 words against a target of 3,400. The script was sent back for expansion and shipped at 3,403 words. The final episode ran 20.4 minutes of audio.

Citation matching fell short on deep dives

Of 15 roundup citations, 12 matched their sources. Of 3 deep-dive citations, 2 matched. The unmatched citations remain in the episode without verification.

Brave API budget exhausted mid-run

The pipeline allocated 3 calls to Brave for body-backfill—summaries of thin articles. All 3 calls were spent. Remaining articles below word count kept their stub bodies and risk being cut from the script as sparse.

The daily anchor framing—"What gets lost when something gets easier?"—had no per-weekday variations in the prompt. Each day frames the question unaided, with no structural diversity across the week.

Gemini render retries added variance

Two sections required retry attempts on Gemini's TTS model. One was retried once, another three times. Both completed successfully, but delivery from those takes may differ from the rest of the episode's voice.

One TTS segment came back shorter than its word count predicted. A retry was generated. The longer of the two takes was kept, so nothing dropped from the episode.
