TTS failure forced a voice split mid-episode

Gemini's multi-speaker endpoint timed out on the welcome section after 5 attempts—1 rejected outright, 4 unanswered. The pipeline fell back to OpenAI for that segment and all remaining audio. The welcome section now carries Gemini's voice while the rest of the episode uses OpenAI's, a discontinuity baked into the shipped file.

The timeout hit generativelanguage.googleapis.com at the 85-second mark. Earlier segments that had already rendered kept their voices; later segments could not.

Article bodies stayed thin; nine segments marked sparse

The Brave API budget for body backfill exhausted after 12 calls. Fifty-three roundup articles were dropped entirely. The remaining articles in the script kept stub bodies—short text that may trigger downstream cuts if the render logic decides they lack enough substance to include.

This is a cascade, not a surprise. The pipeline was forced to choose between fuller articles and fewer articles. It chose fewer.

Citation coverage diverged between formats

Roundup citations matched 11 of 15 sources (73%). Deep dive citations matched 0 of 3. The three deep dive pieces—on map projections, robot vacuums, and survival economics—arrived without traceable source alignment, though they were rendered into audio.

Script expanded to target after first draft fell short

The initial draft came in at 2,554 words against a 3,400-word target. The script was sent back for expansion and shipped at 3,123 words. One AI tell pattern surfaced in quality checks.

Audio and video rendered at matching length

Both audio and video measured 19.7 minutes. The audio file is 18.0 MB; the video is 69.3 MB. The episode reached RSS as entry 226.

Two runs, both completed despite delays
