Gemini voice service failed twice; the pipeline rendered audio on OpenAI instead

The canary deployment for Gemini TTS timed out on two separate attempts. Both the wildcard test and the gemini-2.5-pro-preview-tts instance hit 30-second read timeouts against generativelanguage.googleapis.com. Rather than risk a mid-episode voice switch, the pipeline downgraded to OpenAI for the entire 19.5-minute episode.

This choice preserved continuity at a cost. The spend line shows Claude consumed $10 of the budget while Gemini did not run. The audio rendered at 17.9 MB across 19.5 minutes—standard dimensions—but the listener heard a voice they may not have expected on a day when Gemini was supposed to answer the pre-flight check.
