Four short TTS takes, one kept at full length

The most consequential thing in this run is not a failure but a fallback. Four TTS takes came back shorter than their word count predicts. Each was retried once. Of those retries, four were still short. The pipeline kept the longer of the two takes for each, so nothing is dropped from the episode.

The tradeoff is that a short take is evidence the audio may not match the script. Keeping the longer take preserves the content but does not verify that every word was spoken. The facts do not say whether the four still-short segments are truncated, only that the longer take was kept.
