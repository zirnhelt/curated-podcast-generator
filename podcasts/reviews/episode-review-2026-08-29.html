What the pipeline did, what broke, and what it chose when something broke. Run 33255914006.

Gemini TTS failed pre-flight; OpenAI rendered the whole episode

Four rate-limit errors from Gemini's text-to-speech models triggered the canary check to fail. The pipeline did not attempt a mid-episode voice switch. Instead, it fell back to OpenAI and rendered all 18.6 minutes of audio on a single vendor. The listener heard no interruption. The cost was vendor concentration for one day.

Brave Answers API hit its usage limit mid-run

The Answers API returned a 402 error. The pipeline switched to web snippets for the remainder of script generation. This affected the script/brave-answers segment. The first draft still arrived at 2,252 words, below the 3,400-word target, and was sent for an expand pass. The final script shipped at 2,955 words.

Ten roundup stories stayed out of order after one repair

A sorting failure in script/roundup-order left 10 stories aired after a later block, even after one correction attempt. The pipeline did not retry. Fifteen roundup stories were dropped from the episode entirely. Five deep dives ran instead.

Citation matching fell unevenly across segments

Roundup citations aligned at 13 of 15 matched. Deep dive citations aligned at 2 of 5 matched. The disparity tracks with the segment's relative complexity—the roundup used shorter, more predictable source text. The deep dives included pieces titled "B.C.'s Sikh community on edge after Edmonton attack" and "Artists Commit to the Bit at This Year's Vulva Gala," which required more precise extraction logic.

Three runs, each later than the last

The first completed run arrived 222 minutes late. The second completed 246 minutes late. A third began 293 minutes late and was still in progress at report time. All three triggered on Fallback 2, the 3:05 AM Pacific schedule. The repeating trigger and escalating latency suggest the primary schedule did not complete.

Script generation took 450 seconds; audio took 334

Generation was the slowest step in the pipeline by a margin of 116 seconds over audio rendering. The script required one expand pass after arriving under target. Nine Claude API calls consumed 87,646 input tokens. The expanded final script was 2,955 words. The episode published to RSS as entry 214.
