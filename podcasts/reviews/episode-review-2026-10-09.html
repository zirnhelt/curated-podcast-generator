The territory check ran out of lookups before it finished

The script's territory check is meant to confirm that every nation named in the script is supported by the territory map. This run's check hit its per-run ceiling of 8 lookups and stopped with names still unchecked: "Douglas", "Gook", "Greens", "Prince", "George-North", "Sheldon", "Clare", "Denice", "Bardua", "Randy", "Greens" (again), "News", and "Osoyoos". The ceiling was reached, so those names were never checked. "Greens" and "News" appear to be false hits on common words, but the pipeline did not establish that. It recorded them as unchecked.
