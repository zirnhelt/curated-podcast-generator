  • Indigenous self-determination in a security setting: Greenland and the U.S. military pact.
  • Coastal danger: from a pact on the Arctic to storm tides hitting the Pacific coast.
  • Same tides, farther south: California's record high tides.

Handoff to the deep dive: Who holds the pen when Nations, provinces and Ottawa disagree, and what happens when a Nation sets its own terms on projects and revenue.

Deep-dive question: When First Nations in Canada and the U.S. try to set the terms of major projects and revenue themselves, does a funded seat at the table amount to real decision-making power, or does the power stay with whoever writes the rules, approves the projects and funds the capacity? And for the young people who come home to hold that work, is it a duty to the Nation or a burden the Nation should not have to carry?
