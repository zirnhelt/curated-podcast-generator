What the pipeline did, what broke, and what it chose when something broke. Run 37591489581.

Cohere embeddings offline; fallback paths held

The primary run at 1:05 AM completed successfully, but three separate steps in the script generation stage lost access to Cohere's embedding service. The pipeline detected a ModuleNotFoundError and fell back to keyword-based ranking in all three cases: evolving-story detection, same-story clustering, and deep-dive reranking.

The cost was visible only in the last step. The deep-dive rerank kept its keyword ranking instead of the reranked order. Of the three deep dives that aired — an iFixit teardown, a creatine study, and a Gaza journalism piece — only 1 of 3 citations aligned with sources. The roundup maintained perfect citation matching at 15 of 15, suggesting the keyword fallback worked adequately for shorter stories but degraded on longer investigative pieces.

Script undershoot and expand

The first draft arrived at 1,939 words against a target of 3,400. The pipeline sent it back for one expand pass. The shipped version landed at 2,913 words — still 487 words short of target, but 974 words longer than the draft.

This gap between target and shipped is the largest tension in the run. The audio rendered to 16.9 minutes regardless. No mechanism in the facts states why the second pass stopped short of the full target.

TTS retry loop: two segments still short

Three text-to-speech takes came back shorter than their word counts predicted. The pipeline retried each once. Two of those retries still ran short. The longer take from each pair was kept, so neither segment was dropped from the episode.

R2 sync broke one asset link

During publishing, one object referenced by podcast-feed.xml could not be found in R2 storage and could not be healed from disk. Crawlers requesting that object will receive a 404. The RSS feed points to 252 total episodes; this broken reference is live in the feed.

Voice balance and quality patterns

Casey's airtime exceeded Riley's by a ratio of 1.01 to 1. One AI tell pattern hit during quality review. The script shipped at 2,913 words across 16.9 minutes of audio and 15.5 MB of file size. Video rendered to 16.9 minutes at 58.1 MB.

Roundup pool depth and story cutoff

Fifty-two stories were dropped from the roundup pool by the airtime budget. This is by design — the candidate pool runs larger than a single segment can air, and cut stories can surface on better-matched days.
