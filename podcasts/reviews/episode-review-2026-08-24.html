Gemini's timeout cascaded through the episode

This means the news segment and everything after it came from different models, which may have different delivery characteristics. The canary also logged a 30-second timeout to the same Gemini endpoint, confirming the service was unstable during the run window.

Script expansion worked; audio timing did not

The first draft came in at 2,959 words against a 3,400-word target. The pipeline sent it back for an expand pass. The final script shipped at 3,790 words—390 words over target. The audio produced was 23.6 minutes.

Three TTS segments came back shorter than their word count predicted. Each was retried once. One retry still fell short; the pipeline kept the longer of the two takes, so nothing was dropped. But the duration checksum runs on tts-1's calibration for gpt-4o-mini-tts. Word-omission retries remain uncalibrated until that model is fitted, meaning future short segments may not behave predictably.
