What the pipeline did, what broke, and what it chose when something broke. Run 33393581627.

Gemini's spend cap forced a TTS fallback mid-pipeline

The Gemini project on this pipeline hit its monthly spend cap before rendering began. The system had a fallback ready: use OpenAI instead. But there was a second problem. Gemini's text-to-speech service failed its pre-flight check, so the pipeline could not risk switching voices mid-episode. The entire 18.7-minute episode rendered on OpenAI TTS to keep the voice consistent.

This is the constraint that matters: not money, but the technical requirement that a voice cannot change between segments. Gemini's failure made the fallback mandatory, not optional.

Script came short, expanded, then shipped under target anyway

The first draft hit 2,733 words against a 3,400-word target. The pipeline sent it back for an expand pass. The result was 3,059 words. That is 341 words short of the target, but it shipped. No second expand was attempted.

The shipped script also drew one hit on the AI tell pattern detector. The voice ratio between Casey and Riley was 0.89, meaning Casey carried slightly more of the episode.

Citation alignment broke on the deep dives

The roundup matched 12 of 15 citations. The deep dives matched zero of three. The pipeline pulled three stories: Australia's ban on fully AI-made songs in charts, press freedom in rebel-controlled eastern DRC, and a brutalist LA office conversion. None of their citations were verifiable against the script.

This is also where a Brave search limit became visible. The pipeline had a usage cap of 402 requests. It hit that limit mid-run. Every remaining Brave search was skipped. The anchor script had no per-weekday framings for the debate question either—each day frames it without preparation.

Three production runs, all late, one still running

The first run was manual and 163 minutes late. Three fallback runs followed, each triggered at 3:05 AM Pacific, each arriving 361 to 445 minutes late. The fourth is still in progress. All three completed runs reported success.
