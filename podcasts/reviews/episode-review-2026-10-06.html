Cohere embedding failed three times; pipeline fell back to keyword ranking

The script generation step encountered a ModuleNotFoundError in the Cohere embedding service. This broke three distinct tasks: evolving-story detection, same-story clustering, and deep-dive reranking. The pipeline had fallbacks in place. It abandoned Cohere for keyword-based methods on the first two and kept the keyword ranking on the third without attempting a rerank.

The three deep dives that shipped came from this degraded path. Without Cohere's semantic matching, the selection reflects keyword overlap rather than thematic coherence. The debate question — metrics for measuring success in working-lands industries — aligns loosely with one source (the Farming 4.0 piece) and tightly with none.

Citation alignment fractured in the deep dive

The roundup matched 14 of 15 citations. The deep dive matched only 2 of 3. No source URL appears in the facts for the third deep dive story; the pipeline may have failed to retrieve it, or it may have been generated from a headline without a backing URL at all.

The mismatch splits by segment type. Roundup sources are traceable; deep dive sources are not. This is worth flagging as a data integrity problem independent of the Cohere failure.

Territory check hit its lookup ceiling

The territory-check segment ran eight cross-reference lookups and exhausted its per-run budget. It flagged two named nations (Nlaka'pamux and Tsilhqot'in) as mismatched against the territory map, rejected a rewrite that would have fixed one of them, and skipped checking "Ballot" entirely when it hit the limit.

Two sentences kept an unsupported nation despite the rewrite rejection. The pipeline chose to ship the script rather than halt on a territory mismatch — a choice baked into the design, but one worth noting when a territory check is incomplete.

Script missed its target; expand pass fell short

The first draft arrived at 3,117 words against a target of 3,400. The script went back for expansion. The shipped version — 3,084 words — is 316 words under target and 33 words shorter than the first draft. The expand pass made the script smaller.

This is a reversal. Either the expansion was rejected and reverted, or the second pass involved cuts that outweighed additions. The final word count shipped despite the shortfall.

Audio and video rendered cleanly at 18.7 minutes

Render audio consumed 387 seconds. Render video consumed 108 seconds. Both produced files at expected ratios: 17.2 MB audio and 64.6 MB video for the same 18.7-minute runtime. No degradation flagged.

Feed integrity issue in R2 sync

The publish-to-R2 step found one object referenced in the podcast feed but missing from cloud storage and unrecoverable from disk. This object will return a 404 to feed crawlers. The run completed and published despite the missing asset.
