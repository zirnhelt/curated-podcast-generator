What happened

Two segments aired under budget and one more retry came back short

The first draft of the script came in at 2940 words against a target of 3400, so it was sent back for one expand pass. The shipped script is 3767 words. The pipeline did not truncate to hit the target; it accepted the overage.

Four TTS takes came back shorter than their word count predicts. Each was retried once, and 3 of those retries were still short. The longer take of each pair was kept, so nothing was dropped from the episode, but those three segments may run quieter or faster than the rest. The audio itself is the place to check.

The roundup opened on local news and the deep dive drifted from its charter

The roundup pool kept 18 stories, 14 on theme and 4 local. The airtime budget cut 34 stories from the pool. All 4 local stories aired, and the roundup opened with them. The deep dive led on a charter-aligned item (p100), but the script still retold roundup material: 3 deep dive lines repeated the roundup after polish, including the Arc A750 tokens-per-second example. This is a script-level overlap the polish pass did not remove.

The local-first opening is the designed behaviour, and the cut stories remain in the pool for a later day. The deep dive overlap is the item to act on: tighten the deep dive prompt so it does not re-explain roundup items, or add a check that rejects deep dive sentences that repeat a roundup card's specific figures.
