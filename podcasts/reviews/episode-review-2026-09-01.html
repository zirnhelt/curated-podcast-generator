Gemini's audio render failed mid-episode; OpenAI finished it

The Gemini multi-speaker TTS service timed out on the news section after 120 seconds. The pipeline fell back to OpenAI for the remaining sections and credits. Two earlier canary calls to the same model had failed with 45-second timeouts, signalling the degradation was coming.

The listener hears a tonal shift partway through. Gemini and OpenAI have different voice characteristics and delivery rhythms. The episode shipped anyway because audio continuity ranked below shipping on schedule.

Body-backfill budget exhausted early

The pipeline made 12 calls to expand thin article summaries into full script bodies. Once that budget was spent, 48 roundup articles were dropped rather than shipped with stub text. They do not appear in the final episode.

This is a hard ceiling, not a soft preference. The pipeline chose dropout over filler. The mechanism is visible in the data: 15 roundup citations were available, but only 14 matched into the final script. The one missing citation belongs to a dropped article.

Script expansion worked as designed

The first draft came in at 2,379 words against a 3,400-word target. The pipeline sent it back for expansion. The final script shipped at 3,291 words. One quality flag fired: a single AI-tell pattern hit, caught by post-generation filtering.
