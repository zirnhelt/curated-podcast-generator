Research budget exhausted; web snippets filled the gaps

The pipeline spent its Brave Search deep-dive budget (16 calls) and Brave Answers budget (8 calls) before script generation finished. Later research and fact-resolution queries fell back to Answers or went unanswered. This is the designed failure mode when a story requires more lookups than the budget allows, but it happened early enough to affect three separate deep dives on rural resource policy, critical minerals, and ore sorting.

The script reached 3,192 words after an expand pass—it started at 2,626 words, 774 short of target. The expansion had to work without the full research toolkit. No facts dropped from the episode, but the depth available to support them narrowed as the run progressed.

Territory checks hit ceiling with 8 lookups spent

The pipeline performed 8 territory verifications before reaching its per-run limit. Two matched Indigenous nations to their mapped lands; one sentence kept an unsupported nation because a rewrite was rejected. Eight more place names—Casimir, Tk'emlúps, Stump, Forest, Industries, Central, Rehabilitation, Cariboo-Chilcotin—went unchecked. The story covers rural resource operations across British Columbia, a region where territory attribution carries weight. The names flagged as unparsed were mostly corporate titles and geographic descriptors, not nation names, but the ceiling was hit before the pipeline could confirm that.

Citation alignment nearly complete, one source unmatched

Roundup citations: 14 of 15 matched to source material. Deep dive citations: 3 of 3 matched. One citation—likely from the research phase—could not be traced back to a queried source or snippet. The alignment check flagged it but the episode aired with it intact.

Audio rendered short once, retry held

One TTS segment came back shorter than its word count predicted. A retry was performed. The second take was still short. The pipeline kept the longer of the two, so nothing was dropped from the 20.2-minute episode.

Roundup pool cut to size; 61 stories dropped by design

The curated story pool exceeded the airtime budget by 61 stories. The pipeline selected 3 deep dives and trimmed the remainder. Those 61 stories stay in the archive and can resurface on days where they match better. No editorial judgment is required here—this is the intended overflow mechanism.

One missing asset in R2, crawler will see a 404

An object referenced by the podcast feed is missing from the Cloudflare R2 bucket and cannot be recovered from disk. Crawlers requesting that file will receive a 404. The feed itself published without error, so the break will surface only when a client tries to fetch the missing asset.
