Brave's content-retrieval budget exhausted mid-run

The pipeline spent its allocation of 12 Brave API calls backfilling article bodies and hit the limit before processing finished. Fifty-three stories in the roundup were dropped rather than shipped with stub text. The system's choice: thin articles stay thin, and sparse entries get cut entirely.

This is a capacity constraint, not a quality filter. The roundup citations still aligned at 14 of 15 matched (93%), but the body text that would have explained those citations never arrived. Listeners heard fewer stories as a result.

Gemini multi-speaker TTS failed on the news section

The primary text-to-speech renderer returned an HTTP 500 error during the news segment. The pipeline retried with Gemini's fallback model (gemini-2.5-pro-preview-tts) and succeeded. The section synthesized on retry 1, but it came from a different model than the rest of the episode, so vocal delivery may diverge in that block.

A second fallback retry also occurred on the closing section (the "take") using the same backup model. Nothing was dropped; the episode shipped complete.

One story aired out of intended sequence

The roundup order degradation flagged one story that remained in the wrong position after a repair pass. The episode aired with that story still later than planned.
