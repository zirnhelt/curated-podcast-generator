Kou-Giesbrecht says the researchers wanted to translate the issue into terms the public can grasp. A dollar figure is something a council can set against a budget, and that's a use I'd defend.

**CASEY:** Look at what the dollar figure counts. Wildfire suppression. Net timber yield loss. Nature-based recreation loss. Plus the global damage from the carbon the fires release. That's the whole domestic ledger, and it's three lines wide.
